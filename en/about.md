## Location and Directions

The gîtes are located north of the D-Day beaches, on the east coast of the **Cotentin** peninsula, directly on the seafront, less than 3 km from the small seaside resort of **Quinéville** (Manche department, Normandy region).

Saint-Marcouf-de-l'Isle sits at the heart of the **Parc naturel régional des Marais du Cotentin et du Bessin** — a perfect base for exploring: D-Day beaches, Val de Saire, La Hague, Tatihou Island…

**Key distances:**
- Beach: 20 to 40 m (depending on the apartment)
- Utah Beach and D-Day Museum: ~5 km
- Sainte-Mère-Église (first town liberated): ~10 km
- Saint-Vaast-la-Hougue: ~15 km
- Quinéville (seaside resort): ~3 km
- Cherbourg: ~45 km
- Caen: ~80 km
- Mont Saint-Michel: ~120 km
