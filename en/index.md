A land of sailors, encounters and cross-cultural exchanges, Normandy is heir to a rich and varied history.

You will have the opportunity to visit the sites where world history was made on the **D-Day beaches** — including **Utah Beach** and **Omaha Beach** — as well as the town of **Sainte-Mère-Église** (the first town in France to be liberated, just 10 km from the gîtes).

Normandy is also the land of the **Vikings**, of William the Conqueror, of the Bayeux Tapestry, the Hundred Years' War and the pilgrimages to **Mont Saint-Michel** — places that will leave you with memories and stories to tell for years to come.

## Why Choose Gîte de Mer?

- **Facing the sea**: 20 to 40 m from the beach depending on the apartment (just across the road)
- **Panoramic view**: sea view from every room, from the Cotentin peninsula to the Pointe du Hoc
- **3 adjoining gîtes**: ideal for extended families or groups (up to 15 people)
- **On-site facilities**: sauna, gym, pétanque court, bikes, play area, BBQ
- **Prime location**: 5 km from Utah Beach, 10 km from Sainte-Mère-Église, 120 km from Mont Saint-Michel
- **Holiday vouchers** accepted
