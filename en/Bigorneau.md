### Property Description

**Capacity: 6 people**

Spacious, fully equipped first-floor apartment with **sea view from every room**, open-plan kitchen and living area, 1 separate WC, 3 bedrooms, bathroom with WC, electric heating.

Private external staircase access, very quiet, enclosed garden with lawn, sunny, BBQ, garden furniture, private parking, sauna and gym on the ground floor. **Beach 40 m away.**

### Interior

**Living Room**
- 1 sofa, 2 armchairs
- TV, WiFi

**Open-Plan Kitchen**
- Dining table
- Dishwasher, oven, microwave, washing machine
- Fridge/freezer

**Bathrooms**
- 1 bathroom with shower and WC
- 1 separate WC

**Bedrooms**
- 2 bedrooms with 2 single beds (90 cm)
- 1 bedroom with double bed (140 cm) and private en-suite

**Electric heating**

### Outside

Located facing the sea in Saint-Marcouf-de-l'Isle (just across the road), large enclosed sunny garden with views along the entire east coast of the Cotentin peninsula, from Tatihou to the Pointe du Hoc.

- **Parking**: private, on enclosed grounds
- **Terrace**: outdoor kitchen, Weber gas BBQ, garden furniture
- **Play area**: trampoline and climbing frame
- 3 adjoining apartments on 1 floor
